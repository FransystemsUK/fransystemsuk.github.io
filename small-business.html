A simple website for £150

For a sole trader who needs a proper site and does not want a monthly bill. A tradesperson, a hairdresser, a tutor, a clinic. The trade does not matter. Fransystems designs it. You own it.

One price, then it is yours

£150 covers a simple static website: a few pages that say what you do, where you work, and how to reach you, laid out so it reads on a phone and on a laptop. There is no shop, no booking diary, and no customer login in that price. Those are a different job.

  • £150

    The design and the build. One payment to Fransystems for a simple site.

  • Your name

    The files sit in a GitHub repository opened in your account, not in ours.

  • No monthly fee

    Nothing to pay Fransystems to keep the site online after it is handed over.

You keep the keys

GitHub is the place the website files are stored. Fransystems designs the site and builds it there. The repository is created in an account you own, so the site is not rented back to you.

What Fransystems does

  • Designs the pages around your trade, your words, and your pictures.
  • Builds them as a static site, the same kind of site as this one: plain files, no monthly platform.
  • Publishes them from your repository with GitHub Pages, which does not charge a subscription for a public site.
  • Shows you the account is in your name before the work is called finished.

What you keep

  • The GitHub login, and the repository that holds the site.
  • The right to change it, move it, or give it to someone else.
  • No further invoice from Fransystems just because the site is still up next month.
  • A later edit, if you want one, agreed as its own small job. You can also ask another person, because you hold the files.

A web address such as yourtrade.co.uk is optional. If you want one, you pay the domain company for the name, usually a few pounds a year. That payment goes to them. Fransystems does not add a fee on top, and the site can be reached without a custom name.
