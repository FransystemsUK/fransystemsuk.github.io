Expert advice, ready to build

The advice is specific to Amazon, Microsoft, or both. It is written so a team can build it, including in Terraform, rather than debate it for another quarter.

The questions this is for

  • The accounts no longer match the way the business actually releases a change
  • The network has grown one exception at a time, and nobody wants to touch it
  • An application might belong on Amazon’s own hosting, or on Microsoft’s, and the team wants a decision
